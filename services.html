From the first unsettling detail to the final cinematic impact, we shape audio experiences for horror trailers and independent films.

01 / TRAILER CAMPAIGNS

Make the audience
feel it first.

Horror trailers demand a particular kind of precision. Every breath, impact, transition, and moment of silence contributes to the audience's experience. We build sound with the pacing and emotional arc of the campaign in mind.

The objective is not simply to make everything louder. It's to create contrast, tension, momentum, and a sound that gives a trailer its own identity.

  • Horror and thriller trailer sound design
  • Cinematic impacts, risers, transitions, and designed textures
  • Atmospheric layers, tension beds, and unsettling details
  • Sound editorial and audio polish for trailer campaigns
  • Collaboration with trailer editors, agencies, and production teams

02 / INDEPENDENT FILM

Give the darkness
a voice.

For independent horror filmmakers, sound can transform what an audience sees into something they feel. A carefully designed environment can make a familiar location unsettling, while a restrained sonic detail can make a reveal land harder.

We work with filmmakers to understand the atmosphere, story, and emotional intent of a project, then develop sound that supports the film rather than competing with it.

  • Atmospheric horror soundscapes
  • Creature, supernatural, and environmental sound design
  • Tension-building layers and transitions
  • Sound editing and detail work for selected scenes
  • Creative collaboration with directors and post-production teams

Every detail
has a purpose.

  • Layering and refinement of designed sound elements
